# Site artwork

The navbar bezel, minimap bezel, Quest Hall, and dusk terrace were created with the built-in ImageGen tool. Each uses the Continuum modal kit’s asset overview as a style reference. The optimized frames have transparent centers. The hall and terrace each have desktop and mobile WebP variants. The real event posters, map markers, navigation, and festival information are rendered separately from the artwork.

The runtime files are under `public/continuum/chrome`. The footer wordmark uses the supplied official emblem from `public/continuum/layers`.

## Generation prompts

### chrome-nav

Generate a single wide transparent RGBA decoration for the header of Continuum 2027, matching the supplied voxel event-world artwork and brass/oak ornaments. This is ONLY the decorative bezel for a long floating navigation bar with existing real HTML inside. The frame itself is extremely long and thin, about 10:1 width to height, centered on a large wide transparent canvas. Its outer profile is gently rounded like a pill with small pixel stepped brass endcaps, warm light sandstone edges and a small honey-amber inset square on each extreme end. A delicate thin rail links the left/right endcaps. Keep the entire middle/interior completely transparent so the site can render its own ivory translucent glass and navigation. Transparent background outside too. Refined, light, minimal, subtle handcrafted voxel depth. No labels, logos, words, letters, buttons, compartments, illustration scene, opaque central material, watermarks, or extra ornaments. Do not fill the center.

### chrome-map

A single isolated square voxel cartographer's map bezel for Continuum 2027. Match the provided kit's crisp Minecraft-inspired pixel-stepped stone, dark oak, amber-gold hardware and restrained teal gem accents. Front orthographic view straight on, thin square perimeter frame around a large entirely transparent square opening occupying at least 86% of its width and height. Corner accents only, no dangling decorations. The artwork must be perfectly aligned to square sides and leave the center completely transparent because the website inserts a real map. True transparency inside and outside, zero environmental background. No map, no navigation arrows, no labels, no letters, no number, no branding, no baked UI, no additional objects.

### chrome-gallery

A panoramic 16:9 atmospheric illustration to use behind the event poster gallery on Continuum 2027, in the provided Minecraft-inspired cinematic voxel style. Environment only. A broad medieval castle quest hall built of richly textured block-cut warm sandstone, timber beams and a few softly glowing amber hanging lanterns. Distant arched windows frame a hint of cool blue daylight; subtle carved stonework, vines and floor sunlight. Strong moody but welcoming cinematic lighting. Architecture and details around the edges, large calm central stone wall area for readable real HTML gallery content. Keep the top quarter visually calm, shaded, and low contrast. Art fills entire image. No text, logos, empty poster frames, screens, figures, readable inscriptions, buttons, UI, graphic border, or watermark. This should feel like an inviting hall the visitor discovers after the bright entrance.

### chrome-footer

A cinematic, wide 16:9 environment plate for the Continuum 2027 festival footer. Match the reference kit's handcrafted Minecraft-inspired voxel stone and refined volumetric game lighting. A quiet elevated castle terrace at dusk overlooking distant familiar stone towers and a mountain valley, tiny golden window lights and lantern reflections, deep indigo sky, a low hint of violet sunset and warm amber along the horizon. The distinctive lit towers and lanterns live primarily in the right 55 percent. The left half is calm deep navy sky and shaded block-stone architecture with space for readable live website links. Natural warm/cool lighting, elegant and quiet end to the adventure. Full bleed. No text, sign, people, logos, screenshots, poster, buttons, or layout mockup.
