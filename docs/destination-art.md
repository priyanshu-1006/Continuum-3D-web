# Castle destinations artwork

The four scenes were made with the built-in ImageGen tool. The full scene files are 1600 × 900 WebP; the responsive versions are 960 × 540 WebP. Source PNGs are retained in the ImageGen output directory. Render the live text and interactions in the site; the art contains no labels.

## ImageGen prompts

### Sponsors

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one 16:9 landscape raster environment painting for the Sponsors Hall of CONTINUUM 2027, a premium Minecraft-inspired 3D castle festival website. A grand sandstone great hall, cream block masonry with carved golden cornices, high Romanesque arches, dusty cyan glass, golden lanterns, suspended ivory and amber pixel-cloth pennants without symbols. Three subtly differentiated lighted stone display plinths line the distant right and center-right, with abstract gemstones and small blueprint-like voxel carvings, no logos or words. Architectural storytelling: this is a hall for people who help students build. The left third is quiet, shaded deep warm brown block-stone with softly graded light, suitable for a real headline in front; put the sunbeam and strongest detail on the right. Keep a strong center-right arch visible in a center crop for mobile. Art direction: meticulously hand-built voxel Minecraft blocks and pixel textures, cinematic bounce lighting, warm honey and pale sandstone with teal accents, luminous late morning light, not dark green, no photoreal humans, no text, UI, borders, watermark, or typographic marks. Full bleed single image with visual depth, detailed but calm.

### About

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one 16:9 wide cinematic Minecraft-inspired voxel raster scene for the About page of a castle-based student innovation festival, CONTINUUM 2027. Story: a sunlit open-air castle workshop and observatory where makers share ideas. Layered pale cream sandstone arches, tall blue-enameled tower and beacon, warm copper and cyan contraptions, two small original blocky explorer silhouettes studying a lit workbench at center-right, stairs and lush yellow-green planters below, distant snow-blue mountains behind the tower. Real Minecraft-style pixel surfaces, a sophisticated 3D animated-film composition, clean pale gold and turquoise palette, golden afternoon side light, airy optimism. Left third low contrast darker blue/stone architectural shade for live page title, focal beacon near 65% horizontally for mobile crop; no plain empty rectangle, no panels. No text, lettering, emblems, UI, trademarks, watermark. Single beautiful landscape.

### Contact

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one 16:9 wide cinematic Minecraft-inspired voxel scene for the Contact page of a student innovation festival castle. This is the warm castle dispatch room where visitors can find their way: sandstone and spruce windows, a copper lantern, lit wooden desk with a rolled parchment and red wax seal, a tiny voxel compass, stacked postboxes and a tall arched opening onto the castle's stepped roofs and dusk-blue mountains. Right/center-right: an illuminated doorway and worktable with warm amber and turquoise highlights, focal detail in central 50% for responsive crop. Left third: richly shaded stone and soft gradients, calm enough for live large white text. Image continues full bleed, tactile genuine block geometry and pixel textures, cinematic soft light and vibrant accents, warm not gloomy. No text, writing, runic lettering, numbers, logos, UI, watermark.

### Portal

Use case: stylized-concept. One 16:9 wide full-bleed Minecraft-inspired voxel loading-screen backdrop for CONTINUUM 2027. From ground level looking forward into a majestic sandstone castle waygate at blue hour. Huge centered arch made of pale stone and glowing inset cyan cubes, rich violet and teal portal shimmer beyond, cubic light flecks, copper and gold lanterns, lit square paving stones leading into the gate, night blue edges. Camera symmetric, first-person straight ahead, gateway remains visible in central portrait crop. The lower 35% has softly shaded block paving with gentle falloff, so live light text and a progress indicator can be placed there. Cinematic crafted Minecraft 3D, rich readable palette, pixel texture, no photoreal humans, no HUD, UI, text, letters, logos, watermarks, border. The art alone should feel like traveling to a new castle room.
